Cold Email Playbook for Job Outreach
Prepared on June 5, 2026. The companion CSV is India-focused and contains 100 target companies.

The separate public_recruiting_inboxes.csv contains only official, publicly listed contacts. Start with the first four because their career pages explicitly invite applications or resumes by email. Entrata's address is a general contact, so ask for the correct hiring contact before attaching a resume.

Important
Apply to a matching role on the official careers page first.
Email only addresses that the company publicly invites candidates to use.
For companies without a public recruiting inbox, contact a relevant recruiter or hiring manager on LinkedIn. Do not guess their email address.
Send 5 to 10 personalized messages per day, not the same bulk message to all 100 companies.
Never pay a fee or send bank, card, Aadhaar, PAN, or passport details during early recruiting conversations.
Best Cold Email
Subject: Applied for [Role] at [Company] - [Your strongest relevant skill]

Hi [Name],

I applied for the [exact role title] position at [Company], job ID [ID]. I have [X years / a degree in X] and hands-on experience with [two skills that match the job].

Most relevant to this role, I [one achievement with a number, result, or strong project outcome]. I am interested in [Company] because [one specific sentence about its product, team, or mission].

Would you be open to reviewing my application or pointing me to the right hiring contact?

Resume: [link]
Portfolio/GitHub/LinkedIn: [link]

Thank you,
[Full name]
[Phone] | [City]

Fresher Version
Subject: Application for [Role] - [Degree/Skill] candidate

Hi [Name],

I recently applied for the [role title] position at [Company]. I am a [degree and graduation year] graduate with project experience in [skills].

In my strongest project, I [briefly explain what you built and the measurable result]. The role caught my attention because [specific reason tied to the job description].

Could you please review my application or share who manages hiring for this role?

Resume: [link]
Portfolio/GitHub: [link]

Regards,
[Full name]
[Phone] | [City]

Email to a Public Resume Inbox
Subject: [Role/Function] candidate - [X years or strongest skill] - [Your name]

Hello Hiring Team,

I am interested in [specific role/function] opportunities at [Company]. I have experience in [skills], including [one achievement or relevant project].

I have attached my resume and included links below. I would appreciate consideration for a suitable current opening.

Resume: [link or attached PDF]
LinkedIn/Portfolio: [link]

Thank you,
[Full name]
[Phone] | [City]

Follow-Up After 5 Business Days
Subject: Re: Applied for [Role] at [Company]

Hi [Name],

I wanted to follow up on my application for [role title], job ID [ID]. My experience with [most relevant skill or achievement] appears closely aligned with the role.

I would be grateful if you could review my application or direct me to the appropriate hiring contact.

Thank you,
[Name]

Personalization Checklist
Before sending, make sure the message includes:

Exact role title and job ID.
Recipient's correct name and role.
Two skills copied from the job requirements that you genuinely have.
One quantified achievement or strong project result.
One specific reason you want to join that company.
A working resume and portfolio link.
Simple Tracking Columns
Track: company, role, job ID, application URL, date applied, contact name, outreach channel, date contacted, follow-up date, and response.
