# Attainly Grant Roadmap

This roadmap is for getting Attainly ready for Indian startup grants and early
non-dilutive funding. The goal is not to "look funded"; the goal is to become
credible enough that an incubator or grant committee can say yes.

## Brutal Current Status

Attainly has a working product foundation, but it is not grant-ready yet.

What is strong:

- Live SaaS product with authentication, dashboard, campaign workflow, and demo data.
- Clear AI/product angle: deliverability-first outbound, human approval, ROI ledger, CRM sync, worker automation.
- Technical founder can ship quickly with low burn.

What is weak:

- No incorporated company yet.
- No DPIIT recognition yet.
- No paying customers or pilots proven yet.
- Positioning is still blurry: "AI outbound platform" is stronger than "generic AI startup", but it needs a sharper buyer and measurable outcome.
- Grant applications will be weak unless we show real users, usage, and customer pain.

## Best Grant Path For Attainly

### 1. External Incubator / Student Founder Grant

Priority: highest.

Why:

- Your college does not have an incubation cell, so do not wait for one.
- External incubators can still support student founders.
- Many government grants route through approved incubators, not directly through your college.
- This is still better than applying cold to every national scheme with no mentor/referral.

Action:

- Apply to external incubators under "AI SaaS / B2B productivity / revenue automation".
- Use Attainly's live product as proof.
- Ask specifically for pre-incubation, mentorship, pitch review, and grant routing.
- Target incubators connected to Startup India, MeitY/TIDE, state startup missions, engineering colleges, and technology business incubators.

Target ask:

- Pre-incubation support.
- Mentor letter.
- Help with Startup India/DPIIT recognition.
- SISFS incubator referral later.

Where to apply first:

- Startup India listed incubators that support SISFS.
- Your state startup mission or state innovation council.
- Nearby engineering college incubators, even if they are not your college.
- IIT/IIM/IIIT/NIT technology business incubators that accept external founders.
- Antler India, 100X.VC, WTFund-style young founder programs, and student founder competitions.

### 2. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme

Priority: high, but only after incorporation + DPIIT recognition.

Fit:

- Attainly can fit as a technology-enabled B2B SaaS platform if positioned around AI productivity, sales automation, and measurable SME revenue outcomes.

Blockers:

- Must incorporate first.
- Must get DPIIT recognition.
- Needs stronger traction than just "I built the app".

Best milestone before applying:

- 3-5 pilot users.
- At least 1 paying or LOI-backed customer.
- Proof dashboard showing outreach cost, booked meeting, or qualified lead.

Narrative:

> Attainly helps small B2B teams run safe, auditable, AI-assisted outreach by combining intent signals, human approval, deliverability protection, and ROI reporting in one platform. Unlike generic email tools, it prevents unsafe sending, tracks real unit economics, and gives founders proof of which campaigns generate pipeline.

### 3. MeitY / Digital India / TIDE-style Programs

Priority: medium-high.

Fit:

- Strong if Attainly is positioned as AI SaaS for Indian SMEs and digital productivity.
- Stronger if we show Attainly helps small businesses adopt AI safely in daily revenue workflows.

Blockers:

- Needs a focused use case and measurable societal/economic impact.
- Needs incubator linkage.

Best angle:

- "AI revenue operations platform for small Indian service businesses, agencies, and B2B startups."

### 4. AI / SaaS / SME Digitization Grants

Priority: high.

Fit:

- Attainly is best positioned as a B2B SaaS and AI productivity company.
- The strongest grant framing is SME digitization: helping small teams run safer, measurable customer acquisition without hiring a full sales ops team.
- This is a better fit than forcing the product into healthcare.

Best angle:

- AI for Indian SMEs.
- Safe AI automation with human approval.
- Digital sales productivity.
- Auditable AI workflows.
- Cost-efficient customer acquisition for small businesses.

Avoid:

- Applying to biotech/health-specific grants unless Attainly genuinely becomes a healthcare-specific product.

### 5. Young Founder / Student Grants

Priority: high.

Examples to track:

- WTFund-style under-25 founder grants.
- College entrepreneurship competitions.
- State startup missions.
- Hackathon grants.
- AI/startup competitions.

Fit:

- Very good because you are a student technical founder with a live product.

## Legal Readiness Checklist

Do not incorporate just for vibes. Incorporate when you are ready to spend the
next 12-24 months on Attainly or a tightly related product.

Required before serious grants:

- Pick final company/product name.
- Decide founder ownership.
- Incorporate Private Limited company.
- Open company bank account.
- Apply for DPIIT recognition.
- Create basic cap table.
- Keep simple accounting from day one.

Recommended company object:

> Software development, AI-enabled SaaS products, automation tools, business productivity software, CRM/workflow automation, data analytics, and related technology services.

Avoid making the company object too narrow like only "cold email". Attainly
should stay broad enough to cover AI SaaS, revenue workflows, CRM automation,
analytics, and business productivity software.

## Grant Pitch: Short Version

> Attainly is an AI revenue operations platform for small B2B teams. It helps founders identify high-intent prospects, draft personalized outreach, route messages safely through healthy inboxes, require human approval before sending, sync outcomes to CRM, and prove ROI through a campaign cost ledger. The product is built for teams that cannot afford large sales operations but still need safe, measurable pipeline generation.

## Grant Pitch: Problem

Small B2B businesses need customers, but outbound sales is difficult and risky:

- Generic email automation burns domains.
- AI tools hallucinate or act without audit trails.
- Founders do not know which campaigns actually generate ROI.
- Small teams cannot afford large sales operations.

## Grant Pitch: Innovation

Attainly combines:

- Intent signal ingestion.
- Human-in-the-loop approval.
- Mailbox health-based dispatch.
- AI drafting with audit traces.
- CRM sync.
- Campaign-level cost and ROI ledger.

The innovation is not "AI writes emails"; the innovation is a controlled,
auditable revenue execution loop for small teams.

## Metrics To Collect Before Applying

Must-have:

- Number of users/pilots.
- Number of campaigns created.
- Number of contacts uploaded.
- Number of AI drafts generated.
- Number of approvals/rejections.
- Number of replies or meetings.
- Cost per qualified outcome.

Good grant metric:

> "In pilot testing, Attainly reduced the manual workflow from lead selection to approved outreach from X minutes to Y minutes while preserving human approval and deliverability controls."

## What Not To Say

Avoid:

- "We will be a billion-dollar company."
- "We built an AI agent."
- "We automate everything."
- "No competitors."

Say:

- "We have a working product."
- "We are validating with small B2B teams."
- "We reduce manual GTM work while keeping safety and auditability."
- "We are seeking grant support to complete pilots, compliance, and commercialization."
